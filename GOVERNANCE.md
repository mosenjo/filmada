# Filmada governance

Filmada is shared infrastructure for documenting Madagascar's film and audiovisual field.

## Roles

- **Repository steward:** safeguards the domain, repository and technical continuity. This role does not determine every editorial decision.
- **Maintainers:** review contributions, resolve corrections and guide the directory's scope.
- **Editors:** add and update records through Filmada's editorial interface.
- **Contributors:** suggest additions, corrections, sources or software improvements.

## Editorial decisions

Maintainers aim for documented consensus. Contentious biographical claims, rights disputes and requests from living subjects require review by more than one maintainer whenever practical.

## Corrections

People and organisations documented by Filmada may request corrections. Requests should be acknowledged openly, assessed against sources and resolved without unnecessary delay.

## Sustainability

Filmada should avoid features that require constant administration. The repository, data formats and deployment process must remain understandable and transferable to future maintainers.
