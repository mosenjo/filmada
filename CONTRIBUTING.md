# Contributing to Filmada

Filmada welcomes additions, corrections and source improvements from people committed to documenting Madagascar's film and audiovisual field carefully.

## Ways to contribute

1. Suggest a missing person, film, organisation, resource or opportunity.
2. Correct an existing record and provide a reliable source.
3. Help convert imported source notes into direct citations.
4. Join the editorial group and maintain a section of the directory.
5. Improve the website, accessibility, translations or data tools.

## Editorial expectations

- Prefer first-party or authoritative sources: official film pages, festival catalogues, institutions, production companies, published interviews and reputable reference works.
- Link directly to the supporting source.
- Separate documented fact from interpretation.
- Write respectfully about living people and provide a correction path.
- Do not upload an image unless its reuse rights are known.
- Opportunities must include an official link and, when applicable, an expiry date.

## Review

New contributors may submit changes for review. Trusted maintainers may publish directly. Every change remains visible and reversible through GitHub history.

The public issue templates and contributor application will be added when the repository is published.
